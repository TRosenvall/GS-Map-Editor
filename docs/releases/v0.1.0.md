The first preview of GS Map Editor: make your own version of Golden Sun: The Broken Seal without writing code. Repaint towns, hide chests, write new dialogue, add people and doors, reshape the world map, then press **▶ Play** and walk around in your change.

**This is an early test build, and I'm looking for testers.** It works well on the Mac it was made on, but it hasn't been tried on many others yet. If something goes wrong, please [report it](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor/issues/new?template=bug_report.md). Reports that it worked are just as useful.

## Which download?

- **Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4 and later): `GS-Map-Editor-0.1.0-AppleSilicon.dmg`
- **Intel:** `GS-Map-Editor-0.1.0-Intel.dmg`

Not sure? Apple menu → **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon, "Processor: … Intel …" means Intel.

Needs macOS 11 or later, about 3 GB of free space, an internet connection for the first setup, and [mGBA](https://mgba.io) to play. There's no Windows or Linux version yet.

## Opening it the first time

The app isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS warns about it:

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **GS Map Editor** into **Applications**.
2. Open it from Applications. macOS says it can't check the app: press **Done** (or **Cancel**).
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and press **Open Anyway** next to GS Map Editor. Confirm with your password.

On macOS 14 and earlier you can instead right-click the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. You only do this once.

The first run sets everything up for you in about 15 minutes, mostly waiting. It downloads the game's source from [alchemy](https://github.com/PascalPixel/alchemy) and builds the game on your Mac. No ROM is needed, and none is included.

## What's most useful to test

- Does setup finish on your Mac? Which Mac and macOS version?
- Can you make a change, press ▶ Play, and see it in the game?
- Export a mod and apply it with the Patcher: does it play?
- Anything confusing, wrong, or slow.

## Known issues

- You can't remove a person you've added yet (Undo last save takes it back).
- ▶ Play sometimes starts you somewhere you can't walk from (about 4 places in 200). Pick another point under **Start at**.
- Exporting twice with the same name replaces the earlier patch.
- English edition of The Broken Seal only.
