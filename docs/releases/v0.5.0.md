GS Map Editor makes your own version of Golden Sun: The Broken Seal without writing code. Repaint towns, hide chests, write new dialogue, add people and doors, reshape the world map, then press **▶ Play** and walk around in your change.

## New in 0.5.0

- **Update game source.** If you set up an earlier version, the top bar now offers **Update game source…**. It saves your changes as a backup, moves your copy of the game to the version this editor is tested with, carries your mod across (map edits, chests, imported tile sets and all) and builds the game to check it. If anything can't be carried over, your copy is put back exactly as it was.
- **Bridges.** **Where people can stand** now follows the party onto bridges and other paths that cross over one another, and you can build new ones: in Walkability or Height, pick the layer to paint on (**Ground**, **Middle** or **Overhead**) and the landings and the ways on and off follow by themselves. Where two paths cross, the overlay shows stripes; a click changes the upper one and Shift-click the other.
- **The status bar shows every layer.** It grows a line for each layer that has something under the cursor, and follows Shift as soon as you press it.
- **Rooms that Psynergy and the story open.** Doors a Psynergy opens and bridges the story lowers no longer show as cut off; squares only they open show in teal ("later").
- **Psynergy Stones.** The Chest tool can add, change and remove them, and draws them with the game's own sprite.
- **More heights.** The Height tool now also makes round squares and squares with a slanted edge, two shapes the game's own maps use often, and the overlay covers the whole stretch of screen a ramp or staircase fills.
- **Light glows.** The soft lights some rooms have are drawn faintly, as the game shows them, instead of as bright yellow circles.
- **Arrival points in the right room.** In 91 places that share their code with other rooms, arrival points no longer show another room's positions.
- **Faster and lighter.** The editor uses about a quarter of the memory it did after opening many places, a fast brush stroke on a big place no longer stalls, and **Where people can stand** is quicker.
- **Alchemy `a87775874b`** (2026-11-26). Alchemy now keeps each place as a folder of tables; the editor reads and saves that layout, and still reads copies set up by 0.4.0.

## Which download?

- **Windows 10 or 11 (early test):** `Install-GS-Map-Editor.cmd`
- **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4 and later): `GS-Map-Editor-0.5.0-AppleSilicon.dmg`
- **Mac with Intel:** `GS-Map-Editor-0.5.0-Mac-Intel.dmg`

Not sure which Mac? Apple menu → **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon, "Processor: … Intel …" means Intel.

Everyone needs about 3 GB of free space, an internet connection for the first setup and for **Update game source…**, and [mGBA](https://mgba.io/downloads.html), a free emulator, to play. On Windows, use mGBA's Windows installer.

Installing and opening it the first time are the same as before, and are in the [README](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor#getting-started). On a Mac, the app still isn't signed: the first time, open it, press **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Updating keeps your mods and settings.

## What's most useful to test

- **Update game source…** on a copy you set up with 0.4.0 and have changed: does your mod come across and play as before?
- Bridges you build: does the party walk onto them, across and off in the game the way the editor shows?
- Places opened by Psynergy or the story: do they show as reachable now?
- Psynergy Stones you add: can you pick them up in the game?

## Known issues

- Some shaped squares (curves other than round, and other rare shapes) can still only be copied from the map (⌥-click).
- English edition of The Broken Seal only.

## Full changelog

### Updating
- **Update game source…** appears when your copy of the game is one setup downloaded and older than the version this editor is tested with. Your changes are saved first under a backup, then carried to the new version; places alchemy renamed or reorganised are converted, chests are merged per language, and notes on imported tile sets follow their places. The game is built and checked before the update counts as done; any failure puts the copy back exactly, unsaved files included.

### Places
- Bridges and crossing paths: Walkability and Height draw every layer's ground together, stripe where two cross, and let you pick which one a click changes (the upper one, or the one picked there last; Shift-click for the other). A stroke stays on the layer it started on.
- The Height panel picks the layer to paint on: **Ground**, **Middle** or **Overhead**. Painting a square onto a higher layer adds the landings and the ways on and off, and side walls where needed, in the same Undo step. **Erase its squares instead** takes them away again.
- The status bar adds a line for each layer with something under the cursor, and updates when Shift is pressed or released without moving the mouse.
- Height: **Round** and slanted-edge squares can be painted. Ramps and steps cover the whole stretch of screen they fill in the game.
- **Where people can stand** follows what each room's own code changes as it plays (doors opened by Psynergy, bridges the story lowers); squares only those changes open show in teal.
- Light glows in rooms that blend them are drawn faintly, as the game does; they're shown in full while you paint on their layer.
- A place lists only its own scene tables, so arrival points, events and doors no longer come from another room sharing its code.
- Brush strokes redraw only the columns they touch; opening a place redraws half as often.

### Chests
- Psynergy Stones: add one, turn a chest into a stone and back, or remove it.

### Play
- ▶ Play builds on the current alchemy's window settings.

### Speed and memory
- After opening every place, the editor's memory went from about 1.6 GB to about 400 MB.
- **Where people can stand** is about 40% faster the first time and about 6 times faster after that.

### Game source
- Tested with alchemy `a87775874b`. Places are folders of tables (`header.tsv`, `layers.tsv`, the `grid_*.tsv` files, `cell_tiles.tsv`, `shapes.tsv`, `spots.tsv`); the editor reads and writes them, and every file it saves unchanged comes out byte for byte the same. Copies set up by 0.4.0 still open.
