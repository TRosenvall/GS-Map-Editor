GS Map Editor makes your own version of Golden Sun: The Broken Seal without writing code. Repaint towns, hide chests, write new dialogue, add people and doors, reshape the world map, then press **▶ Play** and walk around in your change.

**Español · Deutsch · Français · Italiano · 日本語:** the editor and its guide now come in six languages. Pick one with the 🌐 menu; the editor starts in your computer's language. Guides: [Español](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor/blob/main/README.es.md) · [Deutsch](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor/blob/main/README.de.md) · [Français](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor/blob/main/README.fr.md) · [Italiano](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor/blob/main/README.it.md) · [日本語](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor/blob/main/README.ja.md)

## New in 0.3.0

- **Six languages.** The whole editor, its messages and its guide in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Japanese. The game you're modding stays the English edition, so place names, people, items and your dialogue stay English. The translations were made with AI help and haven't been checked by native speakers yet: if something reads oddly, please [tell us](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) the wording you'd expect.
- **Where people can stand.** Tick **Where people can stand** at the top, or press **Add a person here…**, and the map is coloured from the game's own walking rules: green where the party can walk up and talk, yellow for talking across a counter, blue for ice the party slides across (hello, Imil), orange for doors and exits someone would block, red where the party can't get near. When a spot has a problem, the editor says why in plain words and offers to move the person to the nearest good spot, or to level the ground up to the path. Dragging a person gets the same check.
- **Undo and Redo for everything.** Undo (⌘Z) and Redo (⇧⌘Z) now take back any change, newest first, from any tool and any place: unsaved painting first, then your last 30 saved actions, even after you close the editor. The editor opens the place that changed. The old "Undo last save" button is gone.
- **Alchemy `0c937e7df0`** (2026-11-11). New installs download that version. If you already set up 0.2.0, your copy keeps working.

## Which download?

- **Windows 10 or 11 (early test):** `Install-GS-Map-Editor.cmd`
- **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4 and later): `GS-Map-Editor-0.3.0-AppleSilicon.dmg`
- **Mac with Intel:** `GS-Map-Editor-0.3.0-Mac-Intel.dmg`

Not sure which Mac? Apple menu → **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon, "Processor: … Intel …" means Intel.

Everyone needs about 3 GB of free space, an internet connection for the first setup, and [mGBA](https://mgba.io/downloads.html), a free emulator, to play. On Windows, use mGBA's Windows installer.

Installing and opening it the first time are the same as 0.2.0, and are in the [README](https://github.com/TRosenvall/GS-Map-Editor#getting-started). On a Mac, the app still isn't signed: the first time, open it, press **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Updating keeps your mods and settings.

## What's most useful to test

- Your language: does anything read oddly, or show in English when it shouldn't?
- Imil and other tricky places: do the "where people can stand" colours match what happens in the game?
- Undo and Redo across several saves and places.
- Windows: does each installer step work, and does ▶ Play open mGBA?

## Known issues

- You can't remove a person you've added yet; Undo takes it back.
- ▶ Play sometimes starts you somewhere you can't walk from (about 4 places in 200). Pick another point under **Start at**.
- Places opened by Psynergy or the story can show as unreachable in **Where people can stand**.
- Exporting twice with the same name replaces the earlier patch.
- On Windows, "I already have a copy" asks you to type the folder's path, and Export and the Patcher save inside Ubuntu, under `GSMapEditor` in your Ubuntu home folder.
- English edition of The Broken Seal only.
