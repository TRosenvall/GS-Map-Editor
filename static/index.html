GS Map Editor

Welcome

Change the game and play your change in a minute:
  1. Open a place from the list on the left.
  2. Pick a tool up top: try Chest and click the ground.
  3. Press ▶ Play.

Events

Click a person to see what they say, or any spot to add a sign or a message.

Doors

Blue cells are doors, red ones lead nowhere. Click a door to see or change where it leads, or click any spot to make a new door.

Chest

Click an empty spot to add a chest, or click a chest to change it. Drag a chest to move it.

Stamp

Drag on the map to copy an area.
